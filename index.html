Project Overview & Scope of Work

Construction of two integrated slope-retention walls totaling approximately 46 linear feet (Run 1: 24' 4", Run 2: 21' 7") at a finished height of 1' 10" (22 inches) behind the main rear concrete slab. This structural barrier mitigates hillside runoff, prevents soil and debris erosion onto the concrete pad, and matches the established profile of the existing parking extension.

Turnkey Scope: Includes all site preparation, trenching, structural wall installation, integrated drainage and aggregate backfill, materials, delivery, and project cleanup.
